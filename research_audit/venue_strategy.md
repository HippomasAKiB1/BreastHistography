## 4. Submission Blueprint: IEEE J-BHI / CBM Positioning

To ensure immediate competitiveness at IEEE J-BHI or Computers in Biology and Medicine, the revised manuscript must strictly conform to their formatting and review standards:

1. **Title Formulation:**
   *Recommended Title:*  
   **"Beyond Naive Test-Time Adaptation: Label-Space Alignment and Head-Aware Adaptation for Multi-Instance Breast Cancer Histopathology"**  
   *(Alternative)* **"Diagnosing and Overcoming Test-Time Adaptation Failures in Multi-Instance Learning for Breast Cancer Histopathology"**
2. **Key Scientific Pillars to Highlight in Abstract & Cover Letter:**
   - *Diagnostic Rigor:* Formal proof and empirical demonstration that naive LayerNorm TTA fails under label-space mismatch due to random head re-initialization.
   - *Algorithmic Innovation:* Introduction of **HAT-MIL (Head-Aware Test-Time Adaptation)**, which couples prototype-based feature anchoring with entropy sharpening to update both normalization and classifier parameters.
   - *Multi-Domain Optical Validation:* Empirical evaluation across physical optical magnification shifts ($40\times \rightarrow 100\times, 200\times, 400\times$), achieving 56% to 68% relative recovery of lost OOD accuracy.
   - *Methodological Hygiene:* Patient-wise stratified splits ($N=82$), 100% verified dataset statistics, zero circular validation reporting, and Edwards' continuity-corrected McNemar statistical significance ($p = 0.0311$).
3. **Open Science & Reproducibility Commitment:**
   - Full open-source release of the codebase, dataset manifest, and pre-extracted feature tensors on GitHub and Zenodo upon publication.
